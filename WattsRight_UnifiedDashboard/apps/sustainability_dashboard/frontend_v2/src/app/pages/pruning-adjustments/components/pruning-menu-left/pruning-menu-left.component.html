What am I looking at?

This dashboard predicts how pruning affects your model's quality, speed, and environmental impact. Adjust the threshold below to explore trade-offs. These are surrogate estimates run a benchmark to validate.

The threshold represents a relative weight magnitude cutoff, not a direct sparsity percentage. Higher threshold = more aggressive pruning. E.g., a threshold of 2 removes weights smaller than 2% of the strongest weight in each layer.
